PulsePlay — Local Music Directory
====================================

Place your MP3 or audio files in this folder to play them in PulsePlay.

Supported Audio Formats:
- MP3 (.mp3)
- WAV (.wav)
- OGG (.ogg)
- M4A / AAC (.m4a)
- FLAC (.flac)

Default Sample Track Paths referenced in js/playlist.js:
- assets/music/song-1.mp3
- assets/music/song-2.mp3
- assets/music/song-3.mp3

Note:
You can also upload audio files directly in the browser using the "Upload Music" button in the PulsePlay interface!
